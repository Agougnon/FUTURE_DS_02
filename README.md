Future Interns - Data Science and Analytics Track
Task 2: Customer Retention and Churn Analysis

Project Overview
This repository contains the solution for Task 2 of the Future Interns Data Science and Analytics internship program. 
-Internship Period: July 28, 2026 – August 28, 2026
-Objective:Analyze telecommunications customer data to uncover patterns of customer churn (désabonnement), calculate retention drivers, and provide actionable business recommendations to reduce customer loss.

Tools and Technologies Used
-Python (Pandas, NumPy for data manipulation and metric calculations)
-Seaborn and Matplotlib (for visual data exploration)
-VS Code (Development Environment)

Key Insights and Findings
Global Metrics:
  -Total Clients Analyzed: 7,043
  - Global Retention Rate: 73.46%
   -Global Churn Rate: 6.54%
Impact of Contract Type (Key Driver):
Month-to-month contracts:Experience an extremely high churn rate of 42.71%, representing the major source of customer attrition.
One-year contracts: Drop significantly to 11.27% churn.
Two-year contracts: Show exceptional loyalty with a minimal churn rate of only 2.83%.

 Business Recommendations
1. Promote Long-Term Contracts: Incentivize month-to-month subscribers to migrate to annual or two-year plans through promotional discounts or added perks.
2. Targeted Onboarding: Implement early engagement campaigns for new customers on monthly plans during their first few months to prevent early churn.

 Repository Structure
"WA_Fn-UseC_-Telco-Customer-Churn.csv": The primary dataset used for the retention analysis.
"churn_analyse.py": The Python script containing data cleaning, aggregation, KPI calculations, and plotting logic.
